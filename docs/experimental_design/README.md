# Controlled baseline and ablation design

The English document `baselines_ablations_v2.md` preserves the latest Task E design (v2, September 22–23). This is the single selected design document; superseded v1 files, implementation-gap notes and access-audit logs are omitted.

The design history explains M, B0, B1, A1 and A2. Actual sample size, replication and inference are governed by the frozen results: DeepSeek 1.0.4; 40 2Wiki questions in five conditions and 20 MuSiQue questions in three conditions; two repetitions; 520 observations. Qwen and older protocol panels are historical sensitivity material, not part of this primary terminal comparison. The old plan's three repetitions, complete 400/200-question matrix, Holm corrections and topology extensions must not be described as completed work. B0/B1 have no communication edges; A1/A2 are only available on 2Wiki.

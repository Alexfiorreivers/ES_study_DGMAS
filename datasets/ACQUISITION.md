# Obtain source data and understand reconstruction limits

1. Obtain the 20210407 2Wiki development data and alias files using the official [release README](https://github.com/Alab-NII/2wikimultihop/blob/main/README.md). Retain its Apache-2.0 notice and separately applicable source-text attribution and licenses.
2. Obtain MuSiQue-Ans v1.0 development data from the official [MuSiQue download instructions](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique/blob/main/README.md). Retain MuSiQue attribution and CC BY 4.0 notice; inherited seed/source material can have separate ShareAlike conditions.
3. Match the supplied pool identifiers and release metadata. Existing adapters document structural transformations but are not a replacement for the authors' human-reviewed corrections. Do not claim that downloading upstream data reconstructs the final audited study inputs byte for byte.
4. Exact historical replay/annotation verification requires the separately preserved study records and their hashes. Ask the corresponding author about lawful access rather than scraping missing inputs from model outputs. No unrestricted access promise is made.

To later publish full passage derivatives, document source attribution, applicable per-component licenses and modifications, separate independently licensed annotations where practical, and check imported quotations/exceptions. A current title URL is an attribution candidate; do not invent historical revision IDs or permissions. The present public release avoids redistributing full passages while this work remains incomplete.

# Amendment: small-domain estimator stability and edge applicability

English companion. Recorded on 2026-10-13 after the first grouped results and before the final report. This addition is not preregistered. Frozen inputs, sampling, weights, human adjudications, and the primary overall HT definition remain unchanged.

1. **Restrict the EdgeExactSourceRetention numerator to the same applicable set.** The existing M2 calculation directly counts source_set_retained_exact in the numerator and retained/rewritten/split/merged in the denominator. A few accepted claim_added/dropped records also carry source_set_retained_exact, so direct calculation could exceed one. Following the original rule that each metric uses its own applicable denominator, the new tables also restrict the numerator. All source-event frequencies are retained separately, including SourceExactOutsideApplicable. This repairs consistency of counting rules; it does not change existing event labels or re-adjudicate their semantics.

2. **Add the audit_difference sensitivity view.** Sparse question/small-stratum sampling in the original HT expansion produces a MuSiQue B0 question-macro CSR estimate of 108.42%, and question-level intervals may also exceed bounds. Retain and report these values without clipping. To show precision when complete candidate auxiliary information is used, add the difference estimator: original exact human component + candidate metric total for the remaining population + `sum_sample w*(human-candidate)`. This linearly corrects candidate error under the same established sampling design. It creates no individual labels for unsampled records and does not upgrade candidate identity.

3. **Report views together without selecting favorable conclusions.** Retain the original exact+HT scope distribution as primary and add audit_difference as candidate-assisted sensitivity. Question/condition comparisons show HT, the candidate baseline, candidates with sampled records replaced, and difference correction. Residual correction can also produce out-of-range estimates in sparse domains; do not clip or call them exact probabilities. Edge proxies were reconstructed after review: difference correction does not confer the design-unbiased guarantee of a candidate fixed before sampling and is only descriptive sensitivity analysis.

4. **Interval limitations remain unchanged.** Resample questions within strata while holding established audit weights fixed. Intervals omit complete second-stage human-audit uncertainty and unknown systematic annotation bias. Do not use them for significance claims, tests, or probabilities that unaudited candidates are correct.
